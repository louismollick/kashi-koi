Round 11: quiz on the dock

The dock idea from round 10, built with your notes. The red answer buttons and the top bar are unchanged, and there's no artist tag over the lyrics. All screenshots are from the simulator, with the test songs swapped for silent audio.

Quiz

  • She fishes from the Home harbor. The art stays anchored to the bottom, so a longer sentence crops sky, never her.
  • The sentence sits in a cream-bordered box over the dock.
  • The combo and its fish sit on a dark panel, so they stay readable on the water and the sandy posts. Fish are orange koi, up to five.

Longer lyrics

Two-line lyrics and two-line answers fit, and so does a miss with the review toast and CONTINUE. The stage shrinks first, and the sentence only scrolls once the stage reaches its minimum height. The answer timer now drains inside the box.

End of a song

  • The next song still starts on its own. Its intro shows the finished song's score in the box, plus its fog tiles.
  • The panel shows the best combo, with a gold koi for a perfect run. A perfect run reads "Perfect".
  • PLAY AGAIN replays the finished song. The summary goes away when the next song's first sentence arrives, or when any other song starts.
  • In listen mode the small card is unchanged except that it now says "Perfect".

Review quiz

The Review quiz uses the same scene. Its end screen used to be a bare "All done for now" label. It now scores the session in the box, like the end of a song.

Left alone

  • Listen mode. A stage there would push lyrics off screen, and listen mode is mostly reading.
  • Home. It already had the harbor, and the review card already overlaps the dock.
  • The end-of-song panel doesn't pause the music, because music should keep flowing. The mockup's paused screen would mean stopping playback at the end of every song.
