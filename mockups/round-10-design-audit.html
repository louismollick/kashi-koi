Changes in the PR

Left is main, right is this branch.

Type with a voice

  • Body text and lyrics use M PLUS Rounded 1c. It covers Japanese, so kana, kanji and English share one soft, round voice instead of SF Pro plus Hiragino.
  • Titles, tabs, numbers and short buttons use Pixelify Sans, a readable pixel face that matches the logo and the art.
  • Lyrics and song titles stay in the rounded face: a pixel face has no Japanese and gets hard to read at furigana size.
  • Italics are gone. The rounded face has none, and a faked slant looked cheap.

Settings, rebuilt

  • Every setting is a row: name on the left, value or control on the right, like the song menu. No more stacks of full-width gray buttons.
  • Answer time is a segmented control with a one-line explanation, instead of a button that cycles through hidden values.
  • The lyrics scan result is three big pixel numbers instead of a run-on sentence.
  • Sync, rescan and album art rows show when they last ran. Errors sit under the section they belong to.

Login looks like the app

  • The harbor and the mascot greet you, with the real logo instead of plain bold text.
  • The form scrolls above the keyboard, so the field you are typing in stays visible.

Small pixel fixes

  • The three round shapes became stepped pixel squares: Home's review arrow, the Review tab badge and the right-answer check.
  • The iOS spinner became three pixel blocks that light up in turn.
  • Home hides "Recently played" until something has been played, instead of a header over nothing.
  • The combo label, Continue, Explain and Undo use the pixel face at a readable size.

B. DotGothic16 everywhere

A Japanese pixel font, so even the lyrics are pixels. The most retro by far. One thin weight only, so titles lose emphasis, and small kanji get blotchy.

C. Silkscreen + Nunito

Arcade caps for titles, Latin-only rounded body. Silkscreen is uppercase-only and wide, so "No limit" wraps and the quiz header truncates. Japanese falls back to Hiragino.

Friendly and very readable, closest to the banner's interface text. No pixel type at all, so the screens lean on the art for character.

Pixel titles over the old SF Pro body. Smallest change and no 10 MB Japanese font, but the body still reads serious next to the art.

Mascot

The headbang sheet was drawn out of order, so she snapped forward, back, back, forward with jumps of up to 72 pixels. The strip now plays the frames back to front and back, one smooth sweep per beat. Frames also step on the UI thread, so quiz scoring can no longer make her stutter. Both strips stay loaded, so the first combo of three doesn't flash.

The light fringe came from the gray backdrop blending into her dark outline, plus gray left inside the chair frame. The sprite script now clears enclosed backdrop and peels the blended edge pixels.

Settings layouts

The PR ships plain rows to stay dense and chrome-free. The alternative wraps each group in a framed panel with a title bar, closer to a game's options menu and the banner's panels.

Still rough

Judgment calls I left alone. Each would change how the app feels, so they are yours.

  • The quiz has a big black void. The mascot floats between the sentence and the answers with nothing behind her. The redesign page puts her on the dock.
  • Slate panels against pure black. The navy cards come from the banner, where they sit on a sunny harbor. On true black they feel like a different game. A wood-and-cream panel style would tie back to the art.
  • The mini player is the only light surface. It matches the banner, but on black it pulls the eye more than the review card does.
  • Hairlines are 1 point. Pixel frames are 2 points, so list dividers look thinner than everything else.
  • Bundle size. M PLUS Rounded is about 10 MB for three weights because it carries kanji. Option E avoids that if size matters.
