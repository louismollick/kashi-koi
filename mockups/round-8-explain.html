Though a sharp afterimage flits through my mind, we can get past it, right?

WORDS
残像 ざんぞう afterimage
脳裏 のうり the back of the mind
GRAMMAR
尖とがったsharp
尖るto be pointed
尖ったpointed (describing a noun)

A past form before a noun often describes a state, not a past event.

掠かすめるけどthough it flits through
掠めるto graze, flit through
掠めるけど…though

けど links two clauses with a contrast, like “though”.

越こえていけるだろwe can get past it, right?
越えるto get past
越えていくto go on past
越えていけるcan go on past
越えていけるだろ…right?

いける is the potential of いく. だろ is a casual だろう asking for agreement.
