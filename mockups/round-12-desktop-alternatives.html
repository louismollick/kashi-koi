Desktop layouts not taken

Round 12 shipped one harbor pane on the right with the browse column beside it and playback along the bottom. These are three other ways the desktop could have gone, drawn at 1440 by 900 with the same art. Each keeps the one rule the redesign is built on: the harbor and the girl appear once.

A. Theater: browse and play are two modes

How it works. Browsing gets the whole window: library grid edge to edge. Pressing the bar (or a song) opens Play: the panorama spans the top, the girl on the dock, lyrics or the quiz in one wide centered column. Trade. Best reading and quiz focus, closest to Apple Music's full screen lyrics. You can't browse and watch lyrics at once, and every switch is a full repaint of the window.

B. The harbor is the window

How it works. The panorama is the app's backdrop, dimmed above the waterline. Browse and lyrics float over it as two dialogue boxes. The bottom bar is drawn as the dock itself, and she sits on it at the left, so playback and the mascot share one strip. Trade. The most game-like and the most charming. Least dense, text over art needs scrims everywhere, and the true black background is gone.

C. Lyrics first, library as a sidebar

How it works. The song is the main column: a harbor strip on top, then big lyrics or the quiz, as wide as the window allows. The library shrinks to a right sidebar of compact rows (search, recents, albums), like a playlist panel. Trade. Puts the learning first and makes lyrics huge. Album grids and artwork, the nicest part of browsing, get squeezed into rows, and with nothing playing the main column is empty.
