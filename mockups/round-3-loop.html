Round 3: the loop

Walk and Sit are now fully separate. Neutral styling here; see round-3-styles.html for six game-style directions.
← Round 2 Walk Sit Styles →
WALK
  • A normal player. Lyrics are always on screen; furigana and translation are display toggles that never affect any score.
  • One button: Lost it, on screen and on the lock screen. A tap records when you got lost, not a verdict on a line. You confirm which line later, in Sit.
  • Nothing you do or don't do in Walk counts toward a song being understood.
Why a timestamp, not a line
You realise you're lost after the line is over, and grabbing the phone takes a few more seconds. Recording "lost around 1:12" and choosing the line later means you're never wrong in Walk.
Fog
The dots are the only Walk visual. No ✓ button, no fog to clear by tapping.
Open
Does a mark also need AirPods / Control Center access? Live Activity covers the lock screen; Siri ("lost it") is possible later.
SIT
  • Three things to do: Sort your Walk marks into lines, Review those lines, Play a song in Meaning Match.
  • Everything in Sit is tested, never self-reported. Review is Meaning Match too, just with clips of your lines from different songs.
  • Each song has a Rank (best Meaning Match run) and a separate, manual Understood mark. Neither depends on Walk.
