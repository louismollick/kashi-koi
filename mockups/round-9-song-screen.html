Today

For reference.

Song info Tap the title in the bar.
Bar Scrub line, prev, play/pause, next.
    1

    Three pages

    Swipe sideways between About, Lyrics and Up next.

    Get there Swipe from the lyrics. Dots under the title show where you are.
    Slim bar Play/pause and next. Hidden on About, which holds the full player.
    Moves Scrubber, prev, speed and loop sentence to About. Actions under the analysis.
    • Lyrics stay the landing page.
    • Every surface is one swipe away.
    • Sideways swipes fight the scrub line and iOS back.
    • Hidden until you try it.
    2

    Expanding bar

    The slim bar grows into a full player, like Apple Music.

    Get there Tap or drag up the slim bar.
    Slim bar Play/pause and next. Progress line stays but no longer scrubs.
    Moves Scrubber, prev, shuffle, speed, loop sentence to the full player. UP NEXT and ABOUT tabs below it.
    • Familiar pattern.
    • Song screen gets the cleanest lyrics.
    • Sheet over a sheet.
    • About is two steps away.
    3

    Detent sheet

    One bottom sheet with peek and half stops. Lyrics shrink instead of hiding.

    Get there Drag the slim bar to half, then full.
    Slim bar The peek stop: play/pause and next.
    Moves Half stop: scrubber, prev, UP NEXT and ABOUT tabs.
    • Lyrics keep following the song while you browse the queue.
    • One surface, three heights.
    • Small lyrics at the half stop.
    • Quiz mode must lock the sheet down.
    4

    Pull-down song card

    A slim song card over the lyrics that unrolls when you pull down.

    Get there Pull the lyrics down or tap the card.
    Slim bar Play/pause and next. Hides while the card is open, since the card has transport.
    Moves Title translation and rank into the card. Transport, stats, actions and Next into the open card.
    • Info sits right above the lyrics it explains.
    • Title translation always on screen.
    • Pull-down today dismisses the song screen. That needs a new home.
    5

    Song menu

    One action sheet, opened from ⋯ or a long-press on any song. Pairs with any other concept.

    Get there ⋯ in the song header, or long-press any song row or tile.
    Slim bar Unchanged by this concept.
    Moves Album, artist, download, understood, clip review, re-analyze, translation source.
    • No layout change.
    • Same actions in Library, Album, Home and the queue.
    • Not a home for the queue or the full player on its own.
    6

    Edge drawers

    About slides in from the left, Up next from the right.

    Get there Swipe from a screen edge or tap the edge tabs.
    Slim bar Play/pause and next. Scrubber and prev live in the About drawer.
    Moves Analysis, stats, actions to the left drawer. Queue to the right.
    • Lyrics stay visible beside the drawer.
    • Quick peek, quick close.
    • Left edge is the iOS back gesture.
    • Drawers are rare on iOS.
    7

    Player first, lyrics mode

    The song screen is a full player whose default mode is lyrics. Modes at the bottom.

    Get there Mode buttons: lyrics, Up next, About, ⋯.
    Slim bar Only on the tab screens: play/pause and next.
    Moves Full transport onto the song screen. Quiz mode shrinks it to the scrub line.
    • Everything on one screen.
    • Mode buttons are obvious.
    • Transport takes about 170pt from the lyrics in listen mode.
    8

    Labelled dock

    A row of four labelled buttons above the slim bar, each opening a sheet.

    Get there Tap ABOUT, UP NEXT, PLAYER or MORE.
    Slim bar Play/pause and next.
    Moves Scrubber, prev, speed and loop sentence into PLAYER. Actions into MORE.
    • Nothing hidden, no gestures.
    • Easy to add a fifth door later.
    • Costs about 56pt of lyrics.
    • Four sheets to keep in sync.
    9

    Harbor route

    The playful one. The song is a boat route with a buoy per sentence.

    Get there Tap the route for the harbor map. Tap the title for Departures.
    Slim bar Play/pause and next. The route replaces its progress line.
    Moves Scrubbing becomes dragging the boat between sentences. Info and actions live under the map.
    • Scrubs by the unit you learn: the sentence.
    • Best run and review marks visible at a glance.
    • Most art to build.
    • Sentence-only scrubbing is coarse inside long sentences.
    10

    Stage select

    Pressing a song opens a song page first, like picking a level.

    Get there Press a song. Mid-song, tap the title.
    Slim bar Play/pause and next.
    Moves Quiz or listen becomes a choice on the stage. Info, stats, actions all on the stage.
    • Info gets a real home.
    • Quiz versus listen becomes deliberate.
    • One more tap before music.
    • Breaks press-to-play.
    11

    Liner notes scroll

    One vertical scroll. Notes above the first line, Up next below the last.

    Get there Scroll the lyrics. NOW jumps back to the sung line.
    Slim bar Play/pause and next. Hold it to scrub and reach prev.
    Moves About and actions above the lyrics. Queue below them.
    • No new navigation at all.
    • Listen mode already has a header slot above the lyrics.
    • Manual scrolling fights auto-centering.
    • Quiz mode has no scroll, so it needs another door.
    →

    My pick

    2 (expanding bar) for the full player and queue, with ABOUT as its second tab.
    5 (song menu) everywhere a song appears.
    9 (route strip) as the progress line on the slim bar and full player, minus the map at first.
    Lyrics and quiz keep the whole song screen. Slim bar: play/pause and next.
