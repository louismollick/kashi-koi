Round 2: lines, not words

  • The unit is the line. The goal is to understand the whole song, phrase by phrase. Word lookup is a tool, not the goal.
  • Two modes, both first-class: Walk (big buttons, tap only if you want) and Sit (focused decoding and games).
  • Translation help is layered from free and literal to smart: dictionary gloss, then Apple's on-device translation, then optionally ChatGPT.

Help Ladder (shared by every option below)

The Sit-mode decode screen. You start with the bare line and climb one rung at a time until it makes sense. The fewer rungs you need, the better the line scores. This gives you a measurable result without trusting your own "I got it".
bare line → furigana → word gloss (yomitan-core, free, exact) → literal (Apple Translation, on-device) → reading (ChatGPT, optional)
Sources
Gloss: yomitan-core parse plus first definition. Free, offline, exact.
Literal: Apple Translation framework. Free, on-device, iOS 18+. Flat but fine for lyrics.
Meaning: Sign in with ChatGPT bills your Plus plan (DevDay, Sep 29). It's new, and it's unclear whether it's open to any app or only launch partners. Fallback is Apple's on-device Foundation Models (supports Japanese, free, weaker).
Why it helps the game
Score = how many rungs you climbed. More honest than self-grading.
Grammar is implicit: you see it in the gloss and the reading, not as lessons you could get wrong.
F

Fog Map

Every song starts covered in fog. On a walk, you tap ✓ when a line makes sense as it plays, or ? when it doesn't. ✓ clears that line. ? flags it for Sit mode, where you decode it with the ladder. The song's map clears up over time.
Walk: line plays → ✓ or ? (or nothing) → Sit: clear flagged lines with the ladder → map clears → song cleared
Feel
Exploration. Uncovering a map, one song at a time.
Good
Walk mode is two buttons, and doing nothing is fine
Walk feeds Sit: what you flagged is your study list
Very simple to build
Bad
✓ is self-reported; easy to fool yourself
One state per line, so no deeper levels after clearing
G

Three Stars (read → hear)

Each song is a stage with three stars, and each star is harder. ★ Decode: understand every line with any help. ★★ Read: understand it from the Japanese alone, no furigana, no help. ★★★ Hear: understand it by ear with the lyrics hidden. This fits an N3/N2 reader: reading is ahead of listening, and sung Japanese is where the gap is.
Sit: ★ decode with the ladder → Walk: ★★ glance at plain lyrics, tap the lines you lose → Walk, eyes free: ★★★ lyrics hidden, tap "lost" when you lose the thread
Feel
A Mario-style star chase. Clear progression with a ceiling you can reach.
Good
Separates reading gaps from listening gaps, and that split is your real problem
★★★ works with the phone in your pocket
Albums and artists as worlds make for natural, low-cost meta-progression
Bad
Still honor-system at ★★ and ★★★
Three star states per line make the model more complex than F
H

Meaning Match

Option C again, at the line level. As a line is sung, pick which translation it is. Wrong answers are translations of other lines from the same song, so there's nothing extra to generate and they're plausible by default. It's an actual test, not a self-report.
Sit: rhythm mode, 3 choices per line, combo → Walk: lock screen shows 2 choices for the current line, tap when you feel like it
Feel
Rhythm game, but testing meaning, which is what you want.
Good
Real test, real score; no honor system
Wrong answers come free from the same song
Works for both Sit (fast, rhythm) and Walk (lock screen)
Bad
Needs a translation for every line up front (Apple Translation could do this once per song)
Once you know a song's lines, matching gets easy from memory
Lock-screen buttons mean native Swift work (Live Activity)
I

Liner Notes (your own translation)

The end product is your own translation of the song. In Sit mode you write it phrase by phrase: tap the phrases, choose from gloss suggestions, or type. Then check it against the machine translation. The finished song plays with your words under each line. Later, this is what you'd share.
Sit: translate phrase by phrase → compare → Walk: listen with your translation as subtitles → song in your collection
Feel
Creative. Fansub-translator energy.
Good
Deepest understanding; a translation is the proof
Produces something worth sharing (phase 2 social, built in)
Bad
Slowest loop; Sit only
Not much of a game
Phrase suggestions need decent chunking

My pick: G as the structure (stars per song, albums as worlds) with the Help Ladder as the ★ screen in Sit mode, F's two buttons as the Walk input for ★★, and H as the Sit-mode game that tests ★★★ for real instead of on trust. I fits as phase 2 when social arrives.
